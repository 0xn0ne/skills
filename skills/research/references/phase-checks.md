# Phase Check Templates

Use these templates for mandatory Phase Check outputs during research.

```markdown
> **Note:** This ratio is a working estimate during research. The authoritative T1/T2 ratio comes from the Trust Badge generated by the script — Phase Check ratios will differ from the final Trust Badge.

- Dimensions with weakest coverage: ...
```

## Progressive Research Reasoning R1 (mandatory)

After each round of searching, write 2-4 paragraphs of reasoning that synthesizes what you just learned. This is NOT a summary of search results — it is your evolving understanding of the problem:

> **这不是技术推理专属，而是研究推进过程中理解深化的记录。** 对于非技术主题，4个维度对应：
> - **阶段一：初始框架建立** — 你最初认为什么（初始认知）
> - **阶段二：数据结构发现** — 数字和规律告诉你什么（关键发现）
> - **阶段三：核心矛盾识别** — 什么出乎意料，和假设相比（反直觉发现）
> - **阶段四：综合判断形成** — 最终结论是什么，为什么可信（综合判断）

For technical topics, the 4 dimensions are:

1. **Bottleneck/Root Cause Analysis**: Based on Round 1 evidence, where is the actual bottleneck or core issue? Does it match the user's assumption?
2. **Algorithm/Strategy Reasoning**: What specific technical approaches emerged as promising? Why? What are their complexity characteristics?
3. **Mathematical/Formal Modeling**: For technical topics, what mathematical relationships or formal models describe the problem space? What formulas or constraints define the solution?
4. **Integration/Architecture Planning**: How would the promising approaches combine into a coherent solution? What are the trade-offs?

These reasoning blocks become the `deep-insights` narrative section in the ledger.

## Progressive Research Reasoning R2 (mandatory)

Update your reasoning based on Round 2 evidence:

1. **Contradiction Resolution**: What contradictions emerged? How do you resolve them? What new understanding does this create?
2. **Refined Mathematical Model**: Update any formulas, constraints, or complexity analyses based on new evidence. Are there edge cases (overflow, boundary conditions, cache effects) you didn't consider in Round 1?
3. **Strategy Refinement**: How has your recommended approach changed? What specific implementation details are now clearer?
4. **Gap Identification**: What critical technical details are still missing? What would you need to search for in Round 3 to fill these gaps?

## Progressive Research Reasoning R3 (mandatory)

Update your reasoning based on Round 3 evidence:

1. **Implementation Architecture**: Based on implementation details found, how would the solution actually work? What tools, libraries, APIs are needed?
2. **Cross-Domain Integration**: What insights from adjacent domains can be applied? How do they change the approach?
3. **Gap Analysis**: What critical details are still missing after 3 rounds? What must be verified in Round 4?
4. **Risk Assessment**: What are the top 3 risks with the current approach? What evidence would mitigate them?

## Final Research Synthesis (mandatory before Phase 4)

Produce a final technical synthesis that integrates all 3 rounds of reasoning:

1. **Final Bottleneck/Root Cause**: What is the definitive answer to "where is the real problem?" with evidence
2. **Optimal Strategy**: What is the recommended approach with specific technical justification (complexity analysis, benchmark data, implementation details)
3. **Complete Mathematical Model**: Final formulas, constraints, boundary conditions, and edge cases
4. **Implementation Architecture**: How the solution components fit together, with specific libraries/tools/APIs
5. **Performance Prediction**: Based on evidence, what performance improvement is realistic? What are the assumptions?

This synthesis becomes the `deep-insights` narrative section.
